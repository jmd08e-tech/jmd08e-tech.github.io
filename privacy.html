Privacy Policy — Dell Daycare Photos

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Dell Daycare Photos is a private automation used by one household on its own computer. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): only emails from one daycare sender, to find the photo links in them. It cannot send, delete, or change any email.
  • Google Photos (add-only): it creates one album and adds those photos to it. It cannot see, change, or delete anything else in the library.

What it does with the data

The photos go into the family's own Google Photos album and nowhere else. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party. The sign-in keys are stored encrypted on the household's own computer.

Stopping it

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps & services).
